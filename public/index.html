A NOTE FROM THE CREATOR

Why I keep
looking closer.

Get to know the creator. Tap a question to reveal the answer.

Who is behind Old World Proof?

I'm the creator of Old World Proof, a project about the stories history leaves behind.

What do you make videos about?

I make videos about the old world, Tartaria, and historical theories that invite a closer look.

What inspires your research?

I'm drawn to old photographs, maps, buildings, and records that leave more to explore.

How do you tell these stories?

I enjoy connecting these fragments into stories that are clear and easy to follow.

How do you approach historical theories?

A striking story should still leave room to examine its sources and context.

Why create ebooks?

That's why I want to bring some of these investigations into ebook form.

What do you hope readers take away?

I hope readers will learn, question, and reach their own conclusions.

How can I be part of the journey?

Thank you for being here and exploring the past with me.

BEFORE YOU DECIDE

A few good
questions.

Tap or click a question to read the answer below.

When will the ebooks be available?

The first ebooks are in preparation. A release date has not been announced yet. Follow Old World Proof on Facebook or YouTube for launch news.

How will purchase and delivery work?

Purchases will be handled through Gumroad. Each live product page will show its price, file format, and delivery information before checkout.

Will I be able to preview a book?

Preview pages are not available yet. When an excerpt is ready, you will be able to open it using the Preview button on the book card.

Are historical interpretations presented as settled fact?

We explore historical records alongside theories and interpretations. A theory is not established fact; disputed questions and the limits of available evidence are part of the discussion.

Where can I follow Old World Proof?

Visit the Old World Proof Facebook page. You can also watch our videos on YouTube @oldworldprooforg.
